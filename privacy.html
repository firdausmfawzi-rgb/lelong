Privacy Policy - LelongBid Ads Dashboard

Last updated: 18 October 2026

1. Who we are

LelongBid is a property marketing business based in Malaysia that advertises bank auction (lelong) properties and home refinancing services. The LelongBid Ads Dashboard is an internal software tool used only by LelongBid to create and manage LelongBid's own advertisements on Facebook and Instagram through the Meta Marketing API.

2. Who this policy covers

The dashboard is not a public app. It is not offered to the public, it has no sign-up, and it does not collect personal information from members of the public. People who see or click a LelongBid advertisement interact with Meta's platforms, and that activity is governed by Meta's own Privacy Policy, not by this tool.

3. Data the dashboard processes

  • LelongBid's own advertising account data obtained from the Meta Marketing API: campaign, ad set and ad settings, ad creatives, and aggregated performance metrics such as impressions, reach, clicks, cost per click, click-through rate and spend. These figures are aggregated and do not identify individual people.
  • Property listing details entered by LelongBid staff (for example auction price, property type and location) used to design ad images and write ad text.
  • Operational logs of the tool's own actions, stored locally.

The dashboard does not request, store or process names, contact details, messages or any other personal information of people who see our ads.

4. How the data is used

The data is used solely to prepare advertisement drafts, publish them to LelongBid's Meta ad account, and review how LelongBid's ads perform so that budgets can be adjusted. Property details may be sent to Anthropic's Claude API to draft advertisement text; no personal data about ad viewers is sent.

5. Storage and sharing

All data handled by the dashboard is stored locally on LelongBid's own computer. It is not sold, rented or shared with third parties, other than the service providers needed to operate the tool (Meta Platforms and Anthropic), each under their own terms and privacy policies.

6. Retention and deletion

Locally stored ad metrics and drafts are kept only as long as they are useful for managing LelongBid's advertising and can be deleted at any time. Because the dashboard holds no personal data about members of the public, there is no user data to delete; any request or question can still be sent to the contact below and will be answered within 30 days.
