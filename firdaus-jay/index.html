About Firdaus Jay

Firdaus Jay is the working name of Firdaus Bin Mohamad Fawzi (also written Firdaus Mohamad Fawzi), an auction property agent in Malaysia.

The Lelong Bid Group list at lelongbidgroup.com covers bank, High Court and Land Office auctions from all over Malaysia and is updated twice a day from public auction notices. It shows the reserve price, the auction date and, where available, the market value.

Buyers can ask for a check of the Proclamation of Sale (POS) and the full cost of a unit before auction day. If there is a charge for any service, it is stated clearly in writing before any decision is made. First-time buyers can read the A to Z guide to the auction process. Call or WhatsApp 019-6522361.

Firdaus Jay is a property agent, not a lawyer or a licensed financial adviser, and does not guarantee any auction outcome.
